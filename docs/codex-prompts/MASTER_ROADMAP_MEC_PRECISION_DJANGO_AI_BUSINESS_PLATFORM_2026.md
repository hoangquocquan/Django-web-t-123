# Master Roadmap - MEC Precision Django AI Business Platform 2026

## Mission

Transform MEC Precision into a Django-based business platform with integrated
local AI intelligence and automation governance.

## Current Completed Foundation

- Django backend migration.
- Django Admin UI.
- Django public website core.
- AWS learning lab.
- Ollama integration.
- RAG Knowledge Assistant.

## Master Rules

- Do not create endless migration phases.
- Do not rewrite completed systems.
- Do not remove working features.
- Do not use external AI APIs.
- Do not allow uncontrolled AI actions.
- Always use isolated branches.
- Always create tests, reports, and review evidence.

## Workstreams

- Workstream A: Django Full Frontend Ownership.
- Workstream B: Sales Business Application.
- Workstream C: CRM System.
- Workstream D: AI Sales Assistant.
- Workstream E: AI Document Intelligence.
- Workstream F: n8n Automation Platform.
- Workstream G: AI Software Factory V2.

## Validation

Each workstream must run:

- `python django_backend/manage.py check`
- `pytest`
- AI Factory review when implementation exists.

## Final Status Rule

`MEC_PRECISION_DJANGO_AI_BUSINESS_PLATFORM_COMPLETE` is allowed only after all
workstreams A-G pass implementation, tests, review, and human merge decision.
