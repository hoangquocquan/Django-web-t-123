# MEC Precision Business Simulation Phase

## Objective

Create fictional demo data and validate the complete business operation flow
across Django Core, Sales, CRM, Knowledge Assistant, AI Sales Assistant,
AI Document Intelligence, n8n Automation, and AI Factory V2.

## Safety

- Do not deploy production.
- Do not use real customer data.
- Do not add new business features.
- Keep human approval required for AI and automation outputs.
